Squat League Privacy Policy

On-device squat coaching, optional Game Center competition, and Apple subscriptions.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Effective date: October 17, 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Squat League for iPhone and iPad (com.mahmutaoata.squatvision). It describes the app’s actual processing and optional Apple-operated services. It does not describe other apps on this website.

Questions or privacy requests: hi@damastechnologies.com.

Camera and movement analysis

Squat League uses the camera only during a workout to detect body positions, count repetitions, and show form feedback. Camera frames and pose landmarks are processed in memory on your device. The app does not record or upload camera footage, photographs, pose landmarks, or detailed form measurements.

You control camera permission in iOS Settings. Without that permission, camera-counted workouts cannot run; your saved history remains accessible.

Data stored on your device

Workout summaries, rep totals, durations, movement statistics, workout dates and their recorded time zones, achievement progress, preferences, and pending Game Center submissions are stored locally. Online workout summaries can also include the opponent’s Game Center display name, match preset, rep scores, and result.

Squat League does not operate a workout-storage backend or enable iCloud synchronization for this history. Separate devices do not combine their rep totals or streaks. Static demo mode uses fictional data in a separate in-memory store.

Optional Game Center rankings and achievements

Solo training and history do not require an app account. Apple operates Game Center authentication, player profiles, friend controls, leaderboards, achievements, and matchmaking.

If you explicitly join ranked challenges, integer challenge rep totals are sent to Game Center and associated with your Game Center profile. Published rankings show your display name, profile picture, rank, and score. Friends is an audience filter; a published score may also appear in Everyone rankings. Ordinary solo workouts do not submit leaderboard scores.

Achievement synchronization is on by default when you connect Game Center and can be turned off for each account. Saved choices to turn it off are retained. Synchronization sends achievement identifiers and percentage progress to Apple, including eligible progress from solo training. Existing unowned history is credited once to the first account that synchronizes.

Apple’s service is governed by Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

Live online squat duels

When you start or accept a duel, Game Center connects you with another player. The app exchanges player profiles, match rules, readiness, clock synchronization information, rep events, accepted scores, and match results with that opponent. Camera frames, body landmarks, depth measurements, and form feedback remain on your device. Squat League does not provide camera streaming or audio/video chat.

Genuine online reps can be saved to local workout history and contribute to eligible squat and streak achievements. They are not added to daily or weekly leaderboard totals.

Sharing achievement badges

You can choose Share badge from an earned achievement’s detail screen. The app creates a badge image on your device containing its artwork, localized achievement title and description, and Squat League branding. The shared image does not include your Game Center account name, camera footage, or workout history. Locked and demo badges cannot be shared as earned achievements.

The iOS share sheet lets you choose a compatible app or another destination. Nothing is posted automatically. A receiving social app or service processes the content you choose to share under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes weekly and monthly Squat League Pro subscriptions through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit on your device to verify subscription access, eligibility for introductory offers, renewals, expiration, refunds, and restoration. The app does not receive payment card details and does not send transaction payloads to a custom subscription server.

Restore Purchases contacts Apple when you explicitly request it and may prompt for Apple Account authentication. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple subscription settings. Clearing workout data or deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Squat League has no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated collection endpoint. It does not request your contacts, precise location, or photo library. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to answer your request.

Apple may process information to operate its App Store and Game Center services under its own policies. This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process ordinary website request information when you visit it. This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Use Settings → Privacy and local data → Clear Local Data to remove locally stored workout history, local achievement progress, milestones, and pending submissions. This action is irreversible. Turning off static demo mode returns you to your separate real history.

Previously published Game Center scores remain until their competition period resets; clearing local history cannot retract them. Previously reported Game Center achievements remain with Apple and may be restored when you synchronize the same account. Turning off achievement sync stops further app-initiated reports. Signing out of Game Center prevents new ranked participation; pending reports can resume if the same account reconnects.

Use Apple’s account and Game Center settings for Apple-held information. For support correspondence or questions about your rights, contact the support address above. We do not sell personal information.

Updates

This page will be updated if Squat League’s data practices change. The dates above identify the current policy. If a future version adds a backend, analytics, or new data sharing, its policy and relevant in-app disclosures must be updated before that processing begins.
