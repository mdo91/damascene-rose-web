## Useful outcomes to explain accurately

- Lecture: obtain a transcript and built-in summary; optionally copy the text into another LLM to draft revision questions or study notes.
- Tutorial: read the transcript and optionally ask another tool to draft a step checklist, checking every step against the source.
- Webinar: summarize the discussion; optionally draft proposed action items externally. Do not invent owners, commitments or deadlines absent from the transcript.
- Interview: identify themes; verify quotations, names and numbers against the recording before quoting.
- Language learning: translate the speech, then optionally create bilingual notes or a glossary externally. AI output needs review.
