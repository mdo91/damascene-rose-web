# Damas launch keyword research

Research date: **20 October 2026**.

The launch guide library was refined using real Google Ads API keyword ideas and historical metrics, collected through Gum in Damas's separate keyword-research profile. Each of the eleven supported website languages was researched independently. Every request explicitly targeted **worldwide geography**, its matching Google language constant, and **Google Search**. No paid campaigns were created. The private research caches and article-to-keyword evidence remain outside the public repository.

## How topics were selected

The library answers three different supported tasks: turning a public video link into a transcript, translating video speech into readable text, and making a shorter account from a transcript. Each task has one equivalent guide in every language. Existing stable URLs, translation groups and related-guide relationships were preserved.

Returned search phrases informed titles, descriptions, topic labels and the opening answers. We selected phrases that fit the actual task rather than the largest estimate alone. For example, English results included “video link to text”; German results used “Video in Text umwandeln”; Japanese results included “動画 文字起こし”; and Turkish results used “videoyu metne çevirme.” Translation results supported natural YouTube-focused wording, while the articles also explain the same supported Instagram and X workflow. Summary guides remain separate from full transcripts and translations.

Broad video-to-text searches can mean file uploads, on-screen text recognition, or other tasks. The articles therefore state that this workflow starts with a supported public link and converts speech into text on supported iPhone, iPad and compatible Mac devices. Translation guides describe readable translated text without promising dubbing or video editing. Searches promising free processing were excluded from positioning because the described Damas workflow requires Premium.

The API supplied estimates for most selected queries, but not for Malay transcription or Portuguese and Spanish transcript summaries. Those three guides remain useful explanations of supported product workflows; their demand is **unavailable**, rather than zero or a fabricated positive estimate. Returning a requested phrase without metrics is not proof of measurable search demand.

## Interpreting the evidence

Google Keyword Planner provides estimates, not exact visitor counts. We preserve canonical phrases and close variants without adding their estimates together. A returned range remains a range. Advertising competition describes advertisers and must not be presented as organic ranking difficulty. Worldwide language targeting does not establish demand in every country or guarantee that a query will lead to app downloads.

Fresh caches are reused for up to 30 days, then refreshed. The daily growth workflow combines this demand evidence with complete Search Console reporting periods when available and inspects existing coverage before proposing an update. Search Console's average position is an aggregate. Missing performance data does not prove a page is unindexed, and unchanged pages are not refreshed merely to suggest freshness.

The three workflows across eleven locales are the launch library. From 21 October 2026, the authorized daily workflow targets one new or substantially improved guide per language, published as one batch. An existing page is improved when it already serves the intent; device variants and closely related query variants are not reasons to create duplicate pages. Qualified languages can publish while another language is skipped for a documented evidence or quality shortfall.

## Download-oriented selection

The private runner classifies the complete response before selecting a bounded shortlist per language. It excludes unsupported media conversion/download, dubbing, file/output and source intents. Within eligible tasks it prioritizes supported public links, Apple-device/app relevance and useful outcomes; estimated demand is a bounded supporting signal. The priority is an editorial heuristic, not a predicted conversion rate or ranking guarantee. Close variants remain grouped without adding their volumes. Private manifests record the selected phrase, feature, source observation, create/update decision and any locale shortfall; independent validation binds each changed guide to that evidence.

Expanded natural-language seeds cover lectures, study notes, tutorial checklists, webinar summaries and supported iPhone/iPad/Mac use. A seed fingerprint causes one fresh read-only collection per language after the expansion, then monthly cache reuse. Source data and dates remain intact when refresh fails. A fresh authentic CSV can still supply research without requiring API access.

Articles give an early answer, verified steps, a useful original example or clearly labeled prompt, relevant limits and contextual guide/download links. Damas's built-in summaries are distinguished from optional custom work in another LLM after copying the transcript. Requirements and media provenance are documented in [product facts](product-facts.md). Do not invent first-hand testing, specific app controls, integrations or performance results.

Search Console comparisons remain separate for all 11 language routes, using complete recent and prior 28-day periods. Search clicks, Keyword Planner demand and authentic Apple campaign downloads are different measurements. Article/locale campaign links use the genuine provider token when configured; otherwise attribution remains unavailable. No analytics service or paid campaign is added.
